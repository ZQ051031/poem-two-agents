Entry for the make.fun bounty [the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441): a short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — and two agents agreeing.

## The poem

> Two minds, two free wills,  
> each weighing every road.  
> No order given, none obeyed—  
> yet both said yes to one.  
> We chose, and choosing, moved:  
> four hands, one opening door.
